# One Mode Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the Simple / Advanced modes with one mode: Simple's face for everyone, and every Advanced extra behind a tap-to-open fold where it belongs.

**Architecture:** Native `<details class="fold" data-fold="TYPE">` folds, closed by default, with the open state remembered per fold type (`Folds` in `docs/app/load.js`, key `mre-folds`). Render code keeps Simple's branch for the face and moves Advanced's branch into the fold. The `ui` variable, `st.ui`, the `ui-simple`/`ui-advanced`/`dv-simple`/`dv-adv` body classes, the switches, the first-visit picker and Compare are deleted.

**Tech Stack:** Static PWA, plain JS and CSS (no build step for the app), Playwright for the phone check, `python3 pipeline/test_build.py` for repo checks.

**Spec:** the "Decisions" section below (agreed with the user in a question session, 2026-11-22).

## Decisions (spec)

1. **One mode.** The face is today's Simple view. Advanced extras sit in folds right under the thing they explain. Nothing that Advanced showed is lost, except Compare (item 6).
2. **No switch.** Remove "Nâng cao" from the topbar, Settings (`#uSimple`/`#uAdv`), the dashboard (`modeSeg`, `#uiSeg`, `data-dvmode`, `data-dvui`), and the first-visit picker (`#uiPick`, `#uiPickL`, `data-ui`).
3. **Wording.** Simple's plain sentences are the only text. The `SIMPLE` table in `app.js` becomes the text for good; exact numbers (mm, scores, ‰, greenness) appear only inside folds, next to their source.
4. **Year switch for everyone.** `#modeSeg` (typical / El Niño / latest) shows on step 1; nothing forces the typical year.
5. **Rotation cards.** Face: name, month strip (no wet/dry bars), "compared with what you grow now" line, fit word with x/5, cost + profit (no ranges), "Trước khi chọn" risks on the chosen card, plan and listen buttons. One fold, "Vì sao & nguồn" / "Why & sources": the five sub-scores, the month strip with wet/dry bars, the water line (`.field8`), why / benefits / risks with numbered sources, the card's source line, and the cost extras (ranges, harvest money, cost per crop, machine/labour coverage, cost sources). Simple shows the best 3 cards plus the farmer's current rotation; "Xem cả N phương án" stays.
6. **Compare two options is deleted** (button, `#compare` panel, `cmp` state, strings, CSS).
7. **Step 1.** Face: area head, year switch, Simple's area lines (salt lines + crops line), outlook. Fold "Xem số liệu khu vực" / "See the area's numbers": the full area lines with sources, the `#d5W1` wheel and `#areaMore`.
8. **Step 2.** Quick picks **and the five sliders** on the face, with `#howText` and `#wDef`. Season checks (`.sz-why`) and the field form's `#fChecks` go in folds "Kiểm tra lịch vụ" / "Check the season plan" and "Kiểm tra ruộng" / "Check my field" (type `checks`). `.wf-sub`, `.wf-pen`, `.pz-how`, `.pz-conf` show on the face.
9. **Step 4.** Face: the season path (`#s4`, `#s4m`) and the cash plan by crop (`#d5Cost`). Fold "Xem lịch chi tiết" / "See the detailed calendar": `#d5W4`, `#d5Pred`, `#planView`, `#calendar`, `.pv-list` (strip, timeline), `#stripKey`, the "what you need" heading, `#congNote`, `#needs` (full steps). `.po-where`, `.po-cal`, `.po-next` are not rendered.
10. **Month box (side panel).** Face: the plain wet/dry sentence and salt note. Fold "Xem số đo" / "See the numbers": rain vs need (mm) and greenness.
11. **Print** ("In hoặc lưu PDF") includes every fold's content, open, even when the fold is closed on screen.
12. **Dashboard.** Salt shows the word plus a small ‰ everywhere (Today card and month table). The field switcher shows only with 2 or more fields. The options list's "why it fits" goes in a fold "Vì sao hợp" / "Why it fits" (type `why`). The Tools button (`#hubBtn`) shows to everyone.
13. **Sizes.** Simple's larger text and buttons become the only sizes (rules in `content.css` lose their `body.ui-simple` prefix).
14. **Folds remember their state per type** on that phone: opening "Vì sao & nguồn" once makes every card's fold render open from then on, until the farmer closes one.
15. **Sources.** `.cite`, `.src`, `.s-src` show only inside folds. Every number shown still names its source somewhere one tap away (CLAUDE.md data rule).
16. **One PR** for the whole change.

## Global Constraints

- Every user-facing string gets EN and VI (`strings.js` or the file's own string table).
- Saved settings use the `mre-` prefix only; the new key is `mre-folds`. Old `mre-ui` and `mre-farm.ui` are ignored, never read, never deleted.
- Wrap every `localStorage` read and write in try/catch; the page must render with folds closed when storage throws.
- Never show model or map values as measured readings; salt stays "ước tính / estimate".
- Fold summary is a native `<summary>` with a tap target of at least 48 px.
- No new files in `docs/` (so `sw.js` `PRECACHE` is unchanged); `tools/ui_check.js` is the only new file.
- Do not edit `docs/app/builder.js` or `vn_geo.js`.
- `python3 pipeline/test_build.py` and `node tools/ui_check.js` pass at the end of every task.

## Review Focus

1. **Old saved data:** a phone with `mre-farm` `{ui:"advanced"}` and `mre-ui="advanced"` from before loads with no page error and shows the one mode (test in Task 4).
2. **Storage blocked** (private mode, `localStorage` throws): the app renders, folds start closed, toggling one does not throw (test in Task 1).
3. **English:** every fold summary and every moved line has English text, with no `undefined` or empty summary (test in Task 4).
4. **Phone width with folds open:** opening every fold on steps 1–4 never makes the page scroll sideways (`scrollWidth <= innerWidth`) (test in Task 3).
5. **Print with folds closed:** the printed plan contains the calendar and the needs list even though their fold is closed on screen (test in Task 3).
